# Evidence: A2 (Lockless BG Thread) + Combos

> 日期: 2026-08-05
> 协议: [[CON-SLA-014]]
> 实现: SPSC per-thread slot + atomic flag + bg thread polling (零 mutex)

## A2 单独结果

### 512MB cgroup (FVC=160)

| 线程数 | baseline | A2 WILLNEED_BG=1 | 差异 |
|--------|----------|-----------------|------|
| 8 | 16,586 | **20,112** | **+21.3%** ✅✅ |
| 12 | 15,691 | **23,482** | **+49.7%** ✅✅✅ |
| **16** | 16,953 | **29,304** | **+72.8%** ✅✅✅✅ |
| 24 | 17,824 | 19,919 | +11.8% |

### 256MB cgroup (FVC=64)

| 线程数 | baseline | A2 | 差异 |
|--------|----------|-----|------|
| **12** | 9,347 | **15,080** | **+61.3%** ✅✅✅ |
| **24** | 11,015 | **14,053** | **+27.6%** ✅✅ |

### A1 (mutex) vs A2 (lockless) 对比

| 场景 | A1 mutex | A2 lockless | 改善 |
|------|----------|-------------|------|
| 512MB 8T | -29.0% ❌ | +21.3% ✅ | **+50.3pp** |
| 512MB 12T | -4.7% | +49.7% ✅✅ | **+54.4pp** |
| 256MB 12T | +14.0% ✅ | +61.3% ✅✅✅ | **+47.3pp** |

**mutex 是核心瓶颈，消除后收益巨大。**
